# BookEater Art Lineage Decisions v1

This document records only visual lineage decisions that have been explicitly approved during concept review. Hidden evolution thresholds remain in code and are not player-facing.

## Canonical starter
- `starter` — 글씨알 / Paperling egg form.
- Approved visual identity: warm ivory egg-like paper body, ink face, dark feet, red bookmark tail.
- Idle must include slow breathing: feet stay grounded while the body rises/falls about 2–3 px with a tiny vertical squash/stretch, plus asynchronous blinking.

## Second growth tier (three sibling routes)
All three are the same growth level. None is an upgrade of another.

- `route_a` — approved layered-book/page creature (previous concept name PAGETTE/PAGEDGE). Fundamental silhouette changes from egg to layered book form.
- `route_b` — approved crumpled-paper shell with dark ink/shadow core (previous concept name SCRIBBLET/INKNEST). Fundamental silhouette changes from egg to rounded crumpled shell/core form.
- `route_c` — approved lantern candidate from concept sheet C-1. Fundamental silhouette changes from egg to lantern form. Keep it simple enough to read as a first evolution.

## Third growth tier — currently approved placement
- `route_a1` — not designed yet. This is the more contemplative / reflective branch of Route A and should receive a distinct silhouette rather than just accessories on Route A.
- `route_a2` — MOVE the previously generated Route C-3 / page-ear scholar creature here. This is now a Route A third-growth candidate. Its larger page-like ears and more characterful silhouette are appropriate for a later growth tier and fit the inquiry/exploration branch better than first evolution Route C.

Other third-growth forms (`route_b1`, `route_b2`, `route_c1`, `route_c2`) remain visually unapproved.

## Art direction rule for all later forms
Evolution must change the creature's fundamental body/silhouette, not merely dress the starter egg in costumes. Accessories and world/theme modifiers are secondary layers. Each approved lineage should retain subtle ancestry cues (ink face, bookmark/red accent, material/shape motifs) while becoming clearly distinguishable in silhouette.

## Current structural tree
- Tier 0: 1 starter
- Tier 1: 3 sibling routes A/B/C
- Tier 2: 2 branches under each route (6 forms)
- Tier 3: 2 final branches under each tier-2 form (12 forms)
- Total: 22 forms

The route topology is stable; species names and remaining art are placeholders until individually approved.
